# Independent evidence on residential/mobile proxy quality (state as of 10 November 2026)

## What recent independent benchmarks or hands-on tests compare success/block rates and latency?

### Takeaway
The strongest current public comparison is Proxyway’s 2026 market study: its transparent, large-scale tests put **Oxylabs and Decodo at the top for baseline residential-network reliability**, with **SOAX close behind**, while **IPRoyal and NetNut regressed materially**. This is not the same as proving low target-site bans: Proxyway’s ordinary CDN success rates were near 99% for many providers, but its Amazon/Google/Instagram aggregate success median was only 74.43%.

### Cited Findings

#### Source quality and test design
- **Proxyway Market Research 2026** is the main evidence base. Most data were collected in **March–April 2026** and the page was updated **23 October 2026**. It covered 13 providers, not including Bright Data. Proxyway discloses affiliate links, gave participants advance access to the methodology, and warns that results may represent participants’ best rather than default configurations. These disclosures make it useful but not conflict-free. — [Proxyway 2026 methodology and report](https://proxyway.com/research/proxy-market-research-2026)
- Proxyway’s residential baseline used a custom open-source Python scraper against a non-blocking ~6 KB global-CDN endpoint: **3.6M global requests over 21 days**, 1.2M each for US and EU pools, 560K each for UK/India/Brazil, and 140K for Australia. IP type was checked with IP2Location, MaxMind and IPinfo; cleanliness checks sampled 20,000 global and 10,000 US IPs per provider using IPinfo and Scamalytics. — [Proxyway 2026 methodology](https://proxyway.com/research/proxy-market-research-2026)
- Its protected-target test was much smaller and deliberately caveated: **5,200 US requests per target/provider across three runs**, at 1 request/second, using HTTPX for Amazon and headless browsers for Google and Instagram. Validation used status code, HTML size and page title. Proxyway explicitly says scraper quality and changing bot protection make this test unreliable in isolation, though useful under identical conditions. — [Proxyway 2026 target methodology](https://proxyway.com/research/proxy-market-research-2026)
- A second hands-on source, **GoLogin (6 October 2025)**, sampled only **50 IPs per provider across the US, Germany and Vietnam**, checking TTFB, stability, Spamhaus, DNS leaks, IP classifications, AbuseIPDB and IPQualityScore. Its small sample, affiliate links, KYC-related exclusions and failure to run several providers through its script make it supporting—not definitive—evidence. — [GoLogin technical comparison](https://test-land.gologin.com/blog/technical-comparison-of-the-top-10-residential-proxy-providers)
- **Shifter’s 2026 tests** are transparent about being single-window, five-country comparisons and use identical request volumes, concurrency, targets and machine. However, Shifter is a direct competitor and prominently compares every provider with itself, so its figures should be treated as **vendor-run secondary evidence**, not independent confirmation. — [Shifter benchmark hub and disclosure](https://shifterx.io/benchmarks); [SOAX review](https://shifterx.io/review/soax)

#### Residential proxies: baseline reliability and latency (Proxyway, March–April 2026)

The figures below are average success and average response time to the non-blocking CDN endpoint. They measure transport/network health, **not target acceptance**.

| Provider | Global success | US success | Global response | US response | Interpretation supported by the report |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| **Oxylabs** | **99.93%** | 99.40% | **0.60 s** | 0.65 s | Best global success; strong all-round latency |
| **Decodo** | **99.92%** | 99.32% | **0.53 s** | 0.75 s | Nearly identical reliability; faster global response |
| **SOAX** | 99.78% | **99.92%** | 0.89 s | **0.55 s** | Best US success; competitive US latency |
| **IPRoyal** | 96.11% | 98.48% | 1.24 s | 1.42 s | Down 3.45 percentage points globally YoY |
| **NetNut** | 95.28% | 98.23% | 1.66 s | 1.27 s | Down 3.12 points globally YoY; slow among leaders |

— All table figures and YoY comparisons: [Proxyway 2026 residential performance tables](https://proxyway.com/research/proxy-market-research-2026).

- Across all 13 tested residential networks, global success median was **99.28%**, US median **99.12%**, global response median **0.93 s**, and US response median **0.80 s**. Byteful was fastest globally at **0.41 s**, Evomi fastest in the US at **0.39 s**, and DataImpulse Premium led UK/EU response at **0.33 s**. Thus Oxylabs/Decodo led reliability, not every latency category. — [Proxyway 2026 key stats and latency table](https://proxyway.com/research/proxy-market-research-2026)
- Oxylabs produced the most unique global residential exits: **3,241,404 unique IPs in 3.6M requests**; Decodo produced the most unique US exits: **961,218 in 1.2M requests**. These observed counts are more informative than advertised pool sizes. — [Proxyway 2026 pool tests](https://proxyway.com/research/proxy-market-research-2026)
- A competitor-run Shifter window reported SOAX completing **96.7–99.6%** in four countries but only **79.3% in the UK** within a 10-second timeout, with response times of **443–534 ms**. This conflicts with Proxyway’s **99.72% UK** success and illustrates window/configuration sensitivity; Shifter itself says it is not a long-term uptime verdict. — [Shifter SOAX test](https://shifterx.io/review/soax); [Proxyway 2026](https://proxyway.com/research/proxy-market-research-2026)

#### Mobile proxies (Proxyway, March–April 2026)
- Mobile baseline tests comprised **1.2M random/global requests over 14 days**, 560K EU, 280K each for US/UK/India/Brazil and 140K Australia. Global success leaders were **Oxylabs 99.84%**, **Decodo 99.75%**, **SOAX 99.74%**, DataImpulse 99.69% and Evomi 99.66%; NetNut was **95.66%** and Infatica 96.59%. Average global response times were Decodo **0.74 s**, Oxylabs **0.76 s**, SOAX **0.92 s**, DataImpulse 1.00 s and NetNut **1.76 s**. Byteful had the fastest global response at **0.48 s**. — [Proxyway 2026 mobile performance](https://proxyway.com/research/proxy-market-research-2026)
- Oxylabs returned **1,192,449 unique mobile IPs in 1.2M global requests**, while Decodo returned **152,424 unique US mobile IPs in 280K requests**. — [Proxyway 2026 mobile pools](https://proxyway.com/research/proxy-market-research-2026)
- Proxyway classified only **75.14%** of NetNut’s and **78.36%** of DataImpulse’s sampled mobile exits as mobile by IP2Location network code, versus 99.04% for Oxylabs, 99.00% for Decodo and 99.28% for SOAX. The report cautions that some vendors label a phone on Wi‑Fi as “mobile,” so database classification and device type are not identical. — [Proxyway 2026 mobile IP composition](https://proxyway.com/research/proxy-market-research-2026)

#### Residential IP cleanliness (supplementary, not a direct ban predictor)
- In Proxyway’s **20,000-IP global sample**, IPinfo identified IPs as known residential proxies for **IPRoyal 16.70%**, SOAX 27.85%, Oxylabs 27.94%, Decodo 29.72% and NetNut 56.09%. Average Scamalytics risk scores were IPRoyal **1.95**, Decodo 2.60, Oxylabs 2.61, SOAX 3.20 and NetNut 5.98. In the 10,000-IP US sample, known-proxy flags rose to IPRoyal 24.25%, Oxylabs 42.89%, SOAX 42.96%, Decodo 47.41% and NetNut 49.24%. — [Proxyway 2026 IP quality tables](https://proxyway.com/research/proxy-market-research-2026)
- Proxyway explicitly **does not fully trust IP checkers** and recommends them only as supplementary evidence. Therefore IPRoyal’s best checker scores do not override its weaker 2026 connection reliability or prove a lower real-world block rate. — [Proxyway 2026 IP-quality caveat](https://proxyway.com/research/proxy-market-research-2026)
- GoLogin’s small 2025 samples found SOAX pools predominantly residential, clean on Spamhaus and without detected DNS leaks; its IPRoyal US pool included transit networks and some IPQS fraud scores of 94–100, while Vietnam had resolution errors/timeouts. This is directionally useful but too small and differently configured to reconcile cleanly with Proxyway’s larger 2026 cleanliness result. — [GoLogin 2025 SOAX/IPRoyal tests](https://test-land.gologin.com/blog/technical-comparison-of-the-top-10-residential-proxy-providers)

#### Bright Data evidence gap and other leaders
- **Bright Data did not participate in Proxyway’s 2025 or 2026 market benchmarks**, so there is no current apples-to-apples 2026 figure for it comparable with Oxylabs/Decodo/SOAX. The 2026 report explicitly notes Bright Data’s absence. — [Proxyway 2026 participants and UX section](https://proxyway.com/research/proxy-market-research-2026); [Proxyway 2025 report](https://proxyway.com/research/proxy-market-research-2025)
- An older Proxyway Bright Data review reported **45.49% total** protected-target success, **85.14% on Home Depot**, and roughly **5% on Amazon** in that historical test, while retaining fast responses. It is evidence that a premium network can still fail badly on a particular target, but its older scraper/target conditions make it unsuitable as a November 2026 performance score. — [Proxyway Bright Data review](https://proxyway.com/reviews/bright-data-proxies)
- Other credible 2026 performers worth including are **Byteful** (best protected-target residential average **81.23%**, fastest global residential response 0.41 s), **Evomi** (fast US response 0.39 s), and **DataImpulse** (best protected-target mobile average **85.31%**). Their smaller market presence does not negate the measured results, but the same target-test caveats apply. — [Proxyway 2026 residential/mobile key stats](https://proxyway.com/research/proxy-market-research-2026)

### Inferences
- The defensible November 2026 shortlist for **demonstrated baseline reliability** is Oxylabs, Decodo and SOAX; Oxylabs and Decodo have the clearest combination of large observed pools, high success and low latency. This conclusion rests on Proxyway’s transparent large tests, not vendor “99.9% success” advertising. — [Source](https://proxyway.com/research/proxy-market-research-2026)
- Bright Data should be labelled **“not enough current comparable public evidence,” not “worse”**. NetNut should not be recommended on its March–April scores without accounting for its July disruption; IPRoyal’s clean checker results and weak connection results measure different things. — [Proxyway 2026](https://proxyway.com/research/proxy-market-research-2026); [NetNut disruption](https://proxyway.com/news/fbi-google-disrupt-netnut)

### Gaps
- No truly disinterested, independently funded 2026 benchmark covering **all seven named providers under one methodology** was found. Proxyway is the closest but uses affiliate links, provider-supplied access and omits Bright Data.
- Proxyway publishes target-test leaders and narrative but its interactive graphs did not expose a full text table of per-provider/per-target success and latency; exact figures for Decodo/Oxylabs on each protected target should not be invented.
- Public tests generally report averages rather than P95/P99 target latency, CAPTCHA rates, challenge rates, account bans or cost per valid response. Those are necessary for a procurement decision.

## Which providers perform best for difficult protected targets versus ordinary web access?

### Takeaway
For difficult targets, **Byteful led Proxyway’s 2026 residential average (81.23%)**, while **Byteful, Evomi, Decodo and Oxylabs clustered around 80%**; **DataImpulse led mobile at 85.31%**, with Decodo fastest at 2.71 s. Ordinary-web winners and protected-target winners differ, and Google was a systemic weak point in Proxyway’s own scraper.

### Cited Findings
- Residential protected-target results across Amazon, Google and Instagram had a **74.43% median success rate**, best **81.23% (Byteful)**, median response **3.21 s**, best **2.58 s (DataImpulse)**. Proxyway says Byteful, Evomi, Decodo and Oxylabs “hovered around 80%,” and that per-target differences were more pronounced than the 10-point spread in provider averages. — [Proxyway 2026 residential targets](https://proxyway.com/research/proxy-market-research-2026)
- Mobile protected-target results had a **75.26% median**, best **85.31% (DataImpulse)**, median response **3.67 s**, best **2.71 s (Decodo)**. At their best, mobile networks succeeded about five percentage points more often than residential networks; DataImpulse, Decodo and possibly Byteful were the only providers to open Google at a “decent rate” with Proxyway’s scraper. — [Proxyway 2026 mobile targets](https://proxyway.com/research/proxy-market-research-2026)
- Proxyway describes Google as having become especially difficult for its scraper. Because the same scraper was used across providers, the comparisons have value, but a low score can reflect scraper/browser quality and changing anti-bot systems rather than IP quality alone. — [Proxyway 2026 target methodology and commentary](https://proxyway.com/research/proxy-market-research-2026)
- Dedicated ISP/static-residential proxies are a different product: Proxyway’s **September–October 2026** dedicated-ISP study found a typical ISP proxy downloaded a 6 KB page nearly **8× faster** and a 2 MB page over **14× faster** than a peer-to-peer residential proxy. Oxylabs reached **40 ms** US and **90 ms** UK small-page responses in favorable server-near-IP setups. These speeds do not establish better acceptance on protected sites. — [Proxyway dedicated ISP study, 9 October 2026](https://proxyway.com/research/isp-proxies-2026)
- The same ISP study says few providers had issues with Amazon but Google remained very challenging at scale; some providers achieved strong Google success, but the text does not expose exact per-provider figures from the graphs. It used ~2,600 US real-target requests and a third-party Amazon scraper plus Camoufox for Google. — [Proxyway ISP real-world benchmarks](https://proxyway.com/research/isp-proxies-2026)
- Historical Bright Data results reinforce target specificity: its older Proxyway review showed excellent speed but only about one in twenty Amazon requests succeeding in that run, versus 85.14% on Home Depot. This cannot establish present performance but directly refutes any universal “premium means almost never blocked” inference. — [Proxyway Bright Data review](https://proxyway.com/reviews/bright-data-proxies)

### Inferences
- For an authorized workload on difficult sites, the most evidence-backed approach is to trial **Byteful, Decodo and Oxylabs** residentially, consider **Evomi** where its geography fits, and include **DataImpulse/Decodo mobile** rather than assuming the largest brand wins. These are test candidates, not universal rankings. — [Basis](https://proxyway.com/research/proxy-market-research-2026)
- Oxylabs/Decodo are safer evidence-based choices when predictable transport and scale matter; Byteful/DataImpulse deserve attention when measured target acceptance matters more than brand size. SOAX has excellent baseline results but less clearly documented protected-target leadership in the text report. — [Basis](https://proxyway.com/research/proxy-market-research-2026)
- Static ISP proxies can be preferable for speed and session consistency on targets that accept them, while peer-to-peer mobile/residential pools may help where network reputation/CGNAT matters. The actual target must decide; proxy category alone does not prove acceptance. — [Residential/mobile target evidence](https://proxyway.com/research/proxy-market-research-2026); [ISP speed evidence](https://proxyway.com/research/isp-proxies-2026)

### Gaps
- No current public test adequately isolates **IP reputation** from browser fingerprint, TLS/client fingerprint, cookies, request sequence and target-specific anti-bot rules.
- No reliable public November 2026 evidence supports ranking Bright Data against these providers on difficult targets.
- “Block rate” is not standardized: connection failure, HTTP 403, CAPTCHA, empty/invalid HTML and account suspension are different outcomes. Proxyway’s target metric combines response code, HTML size and title, but many reviews do not define their usage of “success.” — [Methodology reference](https://proxyway.com/research/proxy-market-research-2026)

## How much do target site, geography, request behavior, rotation, and detection systems affect results?

### Takeaway
They can dominate provider choice. The same report shows ~99% baseline success falling to a ~74–75% protected-target median, major country-to-country changes, and latency determined by gateway/exit/server paths; single-window conflicts between Proxyway and Shifter further demonstrate that no universal ban rate is credible.

### Cited Findings
- **Target and detection system:** Proxyway’s global residential baseline median was **99.28%**, versus **74.43%** on Amazon/Google/Instagram; mobile was **99.63%** baseline versus **75.26%** on the same target set. It explicitly attributes target-test variability to scraper quality and changing bot protection. — [Proxyway 2026](https://proxyway.com/research/proxy-market-research-2026)
- **Geography:** Oxylabs’ residential success ranged from 99.40% US to 99.96% Australia; SOAX from 99.72% UK to 99.92% US; IPRoyal from **93.57% India** to 99.24% Australia; NetNut from 95.28% global to 98.31% Brazil. Response times also varied sharply: IPRoyal averaged **1.42 s in the US**, rising to **2.10 s in Brazil**; Evomi was **0.39 s in the US** but **1.90 s in Australia**. — [Proxyway 2026 country tables](https://proxyway.com/research/proxy-market-research-2026)
- **Conflicting UK result:** Proxyway measured SOAX UK at **99.72%**, whereas Shifter’s single 2026 window measured **79.3%** with a 10-second timeout. Shifter explicitly recommends retesting rather than treating this as permanent. — [Proxyway](https://proxyway.com/research/proxy-market-research-2026); [Shifter](https://shifterx.io/review/soax)
- **Requester/gateway location:** Proxyway placed its global/EU/UK test server in Germany, US/Brazil server in the US, and India/Australia server in Singapore; therefore its response times include those paths and should not be read as universal end-user latency. Its ISP study found California added as much as **300 ms** over Ashburn for US East-Coast proxies; NodeMaven became seven times slower in that comparison. — [Proxyway 2026 methodology](https://proxyway.com/research/proxy-market-research-2026); [Proxyway ISP study](https://proxyway.com/research/isp-proxies-2026)
- **Request rate/client:** The protected-target test fixed **1 request/second** but used different clients by target (HTTPX for Amazon, headless browsers for Google/Instagram), meaning even cross-target averages are not a pure provider-IP comparison. — [Proxyway target methodology](https://proxyway.com/research/proxy-market-research-2026)
- **Rotation/session behavior:** Decodo and Oxylabs default to 10-minute sessions and auto-rotate after 60 seconds of inactivity; SOAX defaults to six minutes and can use strict or “look-a-like” rotation; IPRoyal supports 1 second–24 hours; NetNut keeps an exit only as long as available and lacks the more flexible session options. A rotating exit during a stateful workflow and a stable sticky session are materially different test conditions. — [Proxyway 2026 rotation tables](https://proxyway.com/research/proxy-market-research-2026)
- **Peer uptime:** Proxyway measured sticky-session persistence with **330 US sessions**, requests every 20 seconds, capped at 30 minutes. SOAX led this test, while most Byteful sessions ended around 10 minutes despite documentation saying “as long as available.” — [Proxyway 2026 session persistence](https://proxyway.com/research/proxy-market-research-2026)
- **IP reputation databases disagree with actual acceptance:** Proxyway warns that Scamalytics does not disclose its scoring method and that target sites may not use it. Its dedicated-ISP report likewise says lower-tier IPs do not necessarily perform worse because usage history and scraper setup can matter as much as database classifications. — [Proxyway 2026 IP quality](https://proxyway.com/research/proxy-market-research-2026); [Proxyway ISP quality tiers](https://proxyway.com/research/isp-proxies-2026)
- **Protocol and filters:** Only 3 of 13 residential providers supported UDP by default (with availability caveats for others), and ASN/ZIP/OS filters varied. Proxyway notes gateways concentrated in a few regions significantly affect latency outside the US. — [Proxyway 2026 features](https://proxyway.com/research/proxy-market-research-2026)

### Inferences
- A valid provider trial should hold target URLs, countries/ASNs, client/browser, request pacing, concurrency, timeout, rotation/sticky policy, validation and retry rules constant, and report transport failures separately from HTTP challenges and invalid content. This follows directly from the confounds Proxyway discloses. — [Methodology basis](https://proxyway.com/research/proxy-market-research-2026)
- Report **valid-response rate and cost per valid response**, not just HTTP 200 or infrastructure uptime. Test each priority geography and time window; Shifter’s UK/Proxyway disagreement shows why one run cannot establish permanent reliability. — [Basis](https://shifterx.io/review/soax)
- Rotation is suitable for independent, stateless requests; sticky sessions need separate testing for multi-step workflows. The optimal behavior is workload-specific, and these notes do not recommend bypassing controls on any unauthorized service. — [Rotation feature basis](https://proxyway.com/research/proxy-market-research-2026)

### Gaps
- Public benchmarks do not quantify how much each factor independently contributes; factorial tests varying one dimension at a time were not found.
- Current evidence on the effect of high concurrency, sustained request volume, account history, cookies and behavioral fingerprints is especially weak.

## Are there material user complaints, reliability caveats, or ethical/reputation issues?

### Takeaway
Yes. **NetNut has a severe post-benchmark operational and sourcing/reputation event**: Google/FBI action in July 2026 followed allegations linking its infrastructure to improperly consented proxies and abuse. IPRoyal has a suspicious-ASN caveat and recent blocked-IP complaints; Oxylabs/Decodo have billing/KYC/reliability complaints; SOAX has conflicting reliability reports. Review-platform anecdotes are leads for testing, not population estimates.

### Cited Findings

#### NetNut: material post-test disruption
- On **2 July 2026**, Google said it disabled accounts associated with NetNut control infrastructure, flagged its SDK in Play Protect and shared intelligence; the FBI/IRS seized **netnut.com, proxyjet.io and divinetworks.com**, with netnut.io and alarum.io subsequently taken down according to Proxyway’s 7 July update. — [Proxyway report linking primary sources](https://proxyway.com/news/fbi-google-disrupt-netnut); [Google Threat Intelligence](https://cloud.google.com/blog/topics/threat-intelligence/google-continued-disruption-residential-proxy-networks); [KrebsOnSecurity](https://krebsonsecurity.com/2026/07/fbi-seizes-netnut-proxy-platform-popa-botnet/)
- Qurium research linked the Popa botnet to NetNut infrastructure and reported recruitment through IPTV/streaming/utility apps without proper consent or without consent. Google reportedly observed **316 distinct threat clusters** using NetNut proxies in one week; analysts also criticized KYC and downstream reselling. These are investigation findings/allegations, not a court finding quoted here. — [Qurium “Finding Popa”](https://www.qurium.org/forensics/finding-popa/); [Proxyway synthesis](https://proxyway.com/news/fbi-google-disrupt-netnut)
- NetNut parent Alarum said it was aware of domain seizures, took the matter seriously and would cooperate with law enforcement. — [Alarum statement](https://alarum.io/alarum-technologies-responds-to-inquiry-into-residential-proxy-networks/)
- This event happened **after** Proxyway’s March–April performance collection; September’s ISP report says the DiviNetworks disruption removed the market’s remaining alternative direct-ISP procurement method. Historical NetNut scores therefore cannot establish November availability or stability. — [Proxyway 2026 market report](https://proxyway.com/research/proxy-market-research-2026); [Proxyway September ISP report](https://proxyway.com/research/isp-proxies-2026)

#### IPRoyal
- Proxyway found over **900,000 US IPs** associated with little-known ASNs belonging to Comcast in IPRoyal’s pool; some ASNs became inactive by analysis time. Proxyway said this sounded “suspiciously familiar to BGP hijacking”—a red flag to investigate, **not proof that IPRoyal committed hijacking**. — [Proxyway 2026 pool observations](https://proxyway.com/research/proxy-market-research-2026)
- IPRoyal’s global baseline success fell from 99.56% in 2025 to **96.11% in 2026**, despite leading the report’s third-party cleanliness tables. — [Proxyway 2026](https://proxyway.com/research/proxy-market-research-2026); [Proxyway 2025](https://proxyway.com/research/proxy-market-research-2025)
- Trustpilot’s current IPRoyal page includes October 2026 unprompted complaints alleging IPs were blocked on arrival and replacements/refunds were refused; IPRoyal responded requesting details. These are unverified individual reports and should not be converted into an incidence rate. — [IPRoyal Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/iproyal.com)

#### Bright Data, Oxylabs, Decodo and SOAX
- PCMag’s **30 June 2025** Bright Data hands-on review highlighted enterprise breadth and above-average pricing; its discussion of critical reviews mentioned refund-policy frustration, low-quality ISP/residential proxies and Proxy Manager bugs. These complaints are not quantified or independently benchmarked. — [PCMag Bright Data review](https://www.pcmag.com/reviews/bright-data)
- Bright Data currently requires human-reviewed KYC for residential access and restricts it to verified companies. That explains why GoLogin’s unverified 2025 trial encountered restrictions, but GoLogin’s speculation that Bright Data deliberately supplied “the worst addresses” is **not evidence** and should not be repeated as fact. — [Bright Data access policy](https://docs.brightdata.com/proxy-networks/residential/network-access); [GoLogin test](https://test-land.gologin.com/blog/technical-comparison-of-the-top-10-residential-proxy-providers)
- Oxylabs Trustpilot’s late-2026 snapshot showed **3.9/5 from 774 reviews**, with 12% one-star reviews; themes include positive reliability/support alongside complaints about refunds, unexpected charges, disconnections and ping. Trustpilot also says the company had not recently invited reviews, which may make the sample less representative. These scores are volatile and not performance benchmarks. — [Oxylabs Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/oxylabs.io)
- Decodo’s Trustpilot summary mentions KYC/account deactivation, flagged/blacklisted proxies and some speed/reliability complaints despite many positive reviews. GoLogin could not raise Decodo through its script after about ten attempts even though it worked in the antidetect browser, demonstrating setup sensitivity rather than proving network-wide failure. — [Decodo Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/decodo.com); [GoLogin Decodo test](https://test-land.gologin.com/blog/technical-comparison-of-the-top-10-residential-proxy-providers)
- SOAX’s competitor-run Shifter review found excellent latency but the anomalous UK reliability window; GoLogin found a powerful but overloaded dashboard and clean small samples. These caveats concern consistency and usability rather than proof of systemic poor IP quality. — [Shifter SOAX](https://shifterx.io/review/soax); [GoLogin SOAX](https://test-land.gologin.com/blog/technical-comparison-of-the-top-10-residential-proxy-providers)

#### Sourcing transparency and industry-wide ethics
- Proxyway’s 2026 compliance table identifies Oxylabs sources as **Honeygain, JumpTask and an SDK**, IPRoyal as **Pawns and SDK**, and DataImpulse as **TraffMonetizer, ZoogVPN and SDK**. It describes Decodo’s sourcing only as “reputable providers & a P2P network,” SOAX’s as varied methods including OEM/ODM collaborations with no public detail, and NetNut’s as “Reflection technology” with little public information. Vague sourcing is a due-diligence concern, not proof of misconduct. — [Proxyway 2026 compliance table](https://proxyway.com/research/proxy-market-research-2026)
- The report notes EWDCI/ISO/SOC certifications for several providers but distinguishes certifications from proof that every peer gave informed consent or every customer use is legitimate. Reactive KYC is common; Infatica, NetNut, Proxyrack and Rayobyte were listed as requiring KYC, while Oxylabs/Decodo/IPRoyal/SOAX used reactive approaches. — [Proxyway 2026 compliance table](https://proxyway.com/research/proxy-market-research-2026)
- Proxyway’s market overview describes botnets including BADBOX, Aisuru and Kimwolf, with Kimwolf spreading through residential proxy networks, and Google action against multiple Chinese providers. This makes sourcing and governance material procurement criteria, not merely PR issues. — [Proxyway 2026 market overview](https://proxyway.com/research/proxy-market-research-2026)

### Inferences
- **NetNut requires fresh post-July verification before any procurement**, including legal/status review and a controlled trial; pre-July benchmarks and old review scores are stale for operational risk. — [Basis](https://proxyway.com/news/fbi-google-disrupt-netnut)
- For Oxylabs, Decodo and SOAX, complaints do not erase strong large-scale benchmarks, but they justify testing billing, support, session continuity and the exact target/geography. IPRoyal’s checker cleanliness should be weighed against its reliability regression and unexplained ASN observation. — [Basis](https://proxyway.com/research/proxy-market-research-2026)
- Ethical sourcing claims should be verified by named peer apps/SDK partners, consent/opt-out documentation, abuse handling, KYC and independent incidents—not inferred from pool size, price, ISO logos or review stars. — [Basis](https://proxyway.com/research/proxy-market-research-2026)

### Gaps
- No reliable denominator exists for user complaints, so Trustpilot/G2 cannot estimate the probability of failure or compare true block rates.
- The final legal outcome of the July 2026 NetNut actions and the complete November operational state were not established in the sources reviewed; avoid stating guilt or permanent shutdown as fact.
- No current independent audit proving every provider’s end-to-end peer consent chain was found. Certifications and vendor ethics statements are not equivalent to such an audit.
